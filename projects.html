Chromatic Breach

A roguelite shooter in which a scientist must escape a laboratory overrun by infected enemies. The core mechanic is color-matched combat — players switch their bullet's color to match each enemy's color to land hits; the wrong color means the shot doesn't register as damage.

  • Designed and developed the Reward System — after every wave, the player is offered a choice of 3 randomized rewards drawn from a larger pool.
  • Built the Audio System.
  • Developed a custom toon shader for character and environment outlines.
